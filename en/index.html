Features

What MKW Music can do

MKW Wave

An endless stream tuned to your taste: likes, history, similar artists, time of day and memory of skips.

Synced lyrics

Lines move with the music and the current line glows. Nudge timing with − / + if needed.

Friends

Add friends by nickname, see who is online and what they are playing, and play the same track in one tap.

Smart mixes

Mix of the day, time-of-day mix, forgotten likes, friends are listening, new from your artists.

Voice control

Say “MKW” and a command: next, louder, like, play… 19 command types (Russian speech).

Hand gestures

Nine camera gestures, processed on your device. Works even in the dark.

Profile, top & awards

Custom banner, photo, nickname style, showcase, listener leaderboard and 25 achievements.

Themes

FAQ

Questions

What is MKW Music?

A free music player that runs in your browser and installs on your phone or computer like an app. It has the endless MKW Wave, synced lyrics, friends, smart mixes, voice commands and hand gestures.

Is it free? Are there ads?

Yes, it is free, and there are no ads.

Do I need an email to sign up?

No. Just pick a nickname and a password.

What language is the app in?

The interface is in Russian for now. Music search works for any language.

Where does the music come from?

MKW Music searches iTunes and Deezer (30-second previews), YouTube (full tracks via the official embedded player) and Audius. It does not host any music itself.

Who made MKW Music?

MKW Music is made by MiloKwel, who builds and ships every update.

Start your Wave

Open MKW Music, like a couple of tracks and tap the Wave card.
